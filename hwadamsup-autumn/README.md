# Ready for Autumn 🍁

ASCII autumn-scene animation generator, built for the 재규의세상 YouTube Short
"Ready for Autumn 🍁" (https://youtube.com/shorts/uj_pY4W232c).

## Files
- `hwadam_scene.py` - scene + effects library: perspective corridor of autumn trees in full color, monorail track, front-view car, glitter
- `hwadam_build.py` - final choreography: fast-typing of the real source, variable-speed zones, element-by-element build (the car appears when its def is typed)
- `gen_autumn.py` - compositor and music synthesis: renders frames and audio, assembles the final video

## Output
A 1080x1920 vertical video (35s): terminal/code split view, the 화담숲 corridor building line by line, with synthesized music.
